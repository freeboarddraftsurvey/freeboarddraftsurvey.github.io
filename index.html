Trimming by LCG

Surveyor mode

No ship files? Work straight from the stability booklet. The app tells you which rows to read and interpolates.

Reports in a minute

Draft survey, ballast and trimming reports with your logo, signatures and stamps. Print or save as PDF.

Fully offline
