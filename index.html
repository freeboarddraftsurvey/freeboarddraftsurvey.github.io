About

Built by a ship master

Freeboard is made by Capt. Ramil Apio, master mariner with years on bulk carriers worldwide. The apps started as the tools he needed himself — for the draft survey at the loading port, and the passage plan before sailing.

Every app is free to try, built in simple English, and designed to work where ships work: offline, on a phone, at any hour.

— Capt. Ramil Apio
