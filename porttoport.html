Features

Everything for the passage plan

From the first route to the signed report — appraisal, planning, execution and monitoring in one app.

Route & ETA

The sea route, in seconds.

Choose two ports. Port to Port finds the route through canals, straits and traffic separation schemes, from berth to berth.

  • Great-circle and rhumb-line legs — edit any waypoint
  • ETA at any waypoint, in UTC and ship's time
  • Clock changes, daylight saving and required speed
  • Update the ship's position at sea for a new ETA
▶ Watch: Plan your first route
Weather & storms

See the storm before it sees you.

Tropical cyclones and deep lows with forecast track, cone, dangerous and navigable semicircles — and when they meet your waypoints.

  • Wind, gusts, waves, swell and currents along the route
  • Buys Ballot check with wind and swell arrows
  • Practical steer for your ship type — near the course line
  • Speed-up or wait options with closest approach
▶ Watch: Weather and storms
NAVAREA warnings

Only the warnings that matter.

All NAVAREAs are read, and only the warnings near your route are shown and drawn on the chart.

  • Active, scheduled, expired or until further notice
  • Is it active when the ship passes? The app checks
  • Paste NAVTEX or SafetyNET text — works offline
▶ Watch: NAVAREA warnings
ECDIS

From phone to ECDIS by USB.

Export the route with turn radius and cross-track distance for every leg, and import routes back from the ECDIS.

  • RTZ 1.0 (IEC 61174) — Furuno, JRC, Transas, Kongsberg, Anschütz…
  • GPX, CSV, NMEA 0183 and KML
  • Import RTZ, GPX, KML, CSV or NMEA
▶ Watch: Export to the ECDIS
Passage plan report

A report ready for the Master.

A complete passage plan as PDF: chart with storm track, waypoint schedule, noon forecast, load line, ECA, warnings, checklist and signatures.

  • Storm position against each waypoint, with Buys Ballot
  • Load line zones with dates, ECA and clock changes
  • Print, or save as PDF and send
▶ Watch: Passage plan PDF

Load line zones

Zones and seasonal areas on the passage, with the date you enter each one.

Port details

Depths, entry, pilotage, services and berths — plus your own notes for each port.

Bunkers

VLSFO and LSMGO, ECA change-over, port stays and ROB on arrival.

Vessel position

Report at noon or every watch — run, distance to go and new ETA.

Offline at sea

Save weather, storms, warnings and the live map before you lose signal.

How it works

Four steps to a passage plan

Choose the ports

Departure and arrival, berth to berth. Set speed and departure time.

Check the passage

Weather, storms, warnings, load line and ECA along the route.

Export

Send the route to the ECDIS and make the passage plan PDF.

Monitor at sea

Update the position, save weather offline and re-check every watch.
