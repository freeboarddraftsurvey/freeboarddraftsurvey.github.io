Privacy Policy

Port to Port · Last updated: 19 November 2026

Port to Port is made by Capt. Ramil Apio (Freeboard). This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

What the app stores

Routes, waypoints, the vessel position and time you enter, vessel name and voyage number for reports, fuel settings and other preferences are saved only on your own device. Port to Port has no user accounts and does not send this data to us or to anyone else.

Your location

The app reads the phone's GPS position only when you tap a GPS button (for example to fill in the current position). The position is used on the device and is not sent to us.

Internet services

To show maps, weather and warnings the app downloads public data. Requests contain only map areas or route positions and times — never your name, contact details or device identifiers. Services used:

  • Map tiles: OpenStreetMap, OpenSeaMap, GEBCO and NOAA (US waters)
  • Weather, waves and currents: Open-Meteo
  • Tropical cyclones: GDACS (European Commission / United Nations)
  • Navigational warnings: SeaLagom and NGA, through a copy kept on this website
  • Port facilities: OpenStreetMap Overpass servers
  • Fonts and the Excel / map libraries: Google Fonts and cdnjs

These providers may log technical data such as your IP address under their own privacy policies.

Files you choose to share

When you export or share a file (PDF passage plan, Excel, RTZ, GPX, CSV, NMEA or KML), it goes only where you send it. We never receive a copy. Route files you import are read on the device only.

Ads, tracking and analytics

Port to Port shows no ads and contains no tracking or analytics.

Safety

Port to Port is a planning aid, not for navigation. Always verify routes on official ENC or paper charts, sailing directions and current Notices to Mariners.

Children

Port to Port is a professional tool and is not directed at children.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app or clear its site data to remove all data from your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
